POE iPMS is an intelligent Power-over-Ethernet management platform that monitors, schedules, and optimizes energy use across networked powered devices.

- Discover and inventory PoE endpoints with real-time power draw and port status.
- Enforce per-port power budgets, schedules, and priority-based load shedding.
- Detect anomalies and alert on overloads, failures, or inefficient devices.
- Provide dashboards and APIs for energy reporting and remote control.
- Integrate with SNMP/LLDP and building management systems.
